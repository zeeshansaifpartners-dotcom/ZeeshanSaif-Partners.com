A Note For You ❤️

Zeeshan, sach kahun to har kisi ke saath itna acha connection nahi banta.

Kuch log sirf university tak limited hote hain, kuch sirf class fellows hote hain, aur kuch log dheere dheere apne log ban jate hain.

Tu mere liye unhi logon mein se hai. Tere saath guzara hua time, random baatein, hasi mazaak, pagalpan, choti choti shararatein aur woh saare moments jinhein shayad duniya ke liye koi khaas meaning na ho — mere liye bohat valuable hain.

Sab se achi baat ye hai ke tere saath banda apna asli pagalpan dikha sakta hai. 😂 Na unnecessary formalities, na fake attitude — bas simple si dosti, genuine connection aur har situation mein saath.

1st semester se 7th semester tak… aur hopefully iske baad bhi. ❤️
